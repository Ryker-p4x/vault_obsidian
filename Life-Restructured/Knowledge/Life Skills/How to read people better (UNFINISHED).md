How patrick jane reads people: He notices patterns, changes in them and that gives him the answer.

1. Don't give small gestures meaning inmediately, just use them as more information to use to get the most likely answer.
2. Find how they act normally, their normal body language. After finding it, try to notice any abrupt changes in it.
3. Instead of waiting for your turn to talk and ask questions that are focused on one answer, give people space to talk. Questions like: "Did that upset you?" are focused on one answer, but questions like "How did that make you feel?" allow the other party to give me a more informational answer.
4. Try to understand what the other party actually wants and their motives.
