**Stat priorities by style (most to least important)**

- **Wrestler:** Endurance, Power, Strike Speed, Speed, Evasion
- **Karate:** Strike Speed, Speed, Power, Endurance, Evasion
- **Muay Thai:** Endurance, Power, Speed, Strike Speed, Evasion
- **Scythe:** Power, Speed, Strike Speed, Endurance, Evasion.
- **Unorthodox:** Power, Endurance, Speed, Evasion, Strike Speed. Evasion drops from first place, since his higher Evasion lost the first fight.
- **Boxing:** Strike Speed, Power, Speed, Endurance, Evasion. This rests on one fight, so I’d treat it as provisional.

**Rules for every style**

- Reach is fixed, so you can’t train it. Pick the card for it instead.
- Train the personality’s downside stat first.
- Once a stat is far above the rest, move to the next one on the list.

**Machines:** Pullup Bar, then Treadmill, then Barbell Squat. Speed Bag and Training Log come last.
