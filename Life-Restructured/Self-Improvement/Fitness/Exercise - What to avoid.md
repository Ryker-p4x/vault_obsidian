# Beginner Calisthenics Nutrition

Dietary Framework & Recovery Guidelines

Daily Meal Structure

|Meal|Recommended Components|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|**Breakfast**|Eggs, oatmeal, whole-wheat toast, milk, fruit|Sustained morning energy|
|**Lunch**|Chicken or beans, rice or whole grains, vegetables|Nutrient replenishment|
|**Post-Workout**|Greek yogurt, fruit, cottage cheese, milk|Muscle repair & recovery|
|**Dinner**|Lean protein (fish/beef/poultry), potatoes, mixed greens|Overnight tissue restoration|

Core Nutritional Pillars

Dietary Foundations

- **Protein:** Essential for repairing muscle fiber torn during training.
- **Carbohydrates:** Primary fuel source for training capacity and daily energy.
- **Hydration:** Drink water throughout the day; aim for baseline hydration without sugary drinks.

Sleep & Growth

- **Target:** 8 to 10 hours of uninterrupted sleep every night.
- **Mechanism:** Peak release of tissue-repair hormones occurs during deep sleep stages.
- **Impact:** Inadequate sleep significantly hinders strength gain and recovery.

Items to Limit or Avoid

Restrictions & Reductions

- **Energy Drinks & High Caffeine:** Avoid high-stimulant drinks. They disrupt sleep quality and hydration levels.
- **Excessive Processed Sugars:** Limit soda, candy, and heavily processed baked goods which cause energy spikes and crashes.
- **Caloric Deficits:** Do not restrict total food intake; expanding bodies require sufficient energy to support natural development and training.
