1. Choose concept to learn
2. Consume it, instantly do a project with it with your notes
3. Next day, do another project but this time without your notes

If you can't remember something, stay a few mins trying to force your brain to remember it and if you can't, go back to those notes.

When you're reading something, question it in its entirety, understand why this and why that and just get incredibly obsessed with understanding it.

To learn low level stuff like GCC or CMAKE you can just search up for "Awesome" guides in github.
Also search for highly technical focused blogs on the topic of pros talking about their experience.
Instead of searching for guides search for talks or conferences about the topic or something to do with the topoic. 
For programming languages view video overviews + project building.
For computer science theory, reading books + academic lectures.
